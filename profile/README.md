## Tellurion

A one-person studio in Vienna: two learning apps for the Mac, and free tools that run on your own device.

**Apps**

- [LoreLeaf](https://tellurion.at/loreleaf/): any topic as a skill tree that grows as you learn.
- [LearnHub Studio](https://tellurion.at/learnhub-studio/): course material in, one offline study hub out.

**Free tools**

- [bifolium](https://github.com/bytellurion/bifolium): compare two versions of a PDF side by side, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Try it at [bifolium.dev](https://bifolium.dev).
- [Learning-Hub Builder](https://github.com/bytellurion/learning-hub-builder): turn course material into one HTML file to study from, with any strong AI model.

No accounts and no tracking. The apps are a one-time purchase. More at [tellurion.at](https://tellurion.at) · [Support the free tools](https://ko-fi.com/tellurion)
